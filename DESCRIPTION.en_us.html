Odra Sans is a warm geometric sans-serif for screens, interfaces and brands. Its single-storey a, open single-storey g with a flat, angled tail, angled terminals and generous x-height give it a confident, friendly voice in headlines and clear readability in text.

The family has seven weights, from Light to Black, each with a matching italic, and is available as a variable font with a weight axis. The name comes from Odra, the historic name of Odisha in India.
